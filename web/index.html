Your Mac

Checking your setup…

Choose local speech or OpenAI with your Wolt before setup. With OpenAI, call audio and reply text go to OpenAI and API charges apply. All voices are AI-generated.

2

Get your phone ready

Install Linphone and Tailscale. Connect your phone and Mac to the same Tailscale network.

Your Wolt will guide any sign-in or permission step. Use headphones for your first call.

3

Scan in Linphone

Open this page on your Mac, then use Linphone’s QR scanner on your phone.

This replaces Linphone’s first SIP account. Use a fresh setup or an account you intend to replace. Keep the QR private; it expires after 15 minutes.

Keep Tailscale connected while scanning.

4

Almost ready

Start the phone above. We’ll check the services before you call.

If audio does not connect, dial 100 for an echo test and tell your Wolt what happened. Keep this Mac awake. Stopping the app in the lodge ends its phone services.

Something confusing? Tell your Wolt. It will record setup friction locally and ask before sharing a sanitized report.
Call reliability and car-noise handling are still experimental.
